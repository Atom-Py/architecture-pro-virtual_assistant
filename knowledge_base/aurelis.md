# Aurelis

Aurelis (pronounced /'kɔɹəsɑnt/), also known as Jewel of the Inner Reach, or Dominion Center during the Dominion Era, was an ecumenopolis planet located in the Aurelis system of the Aurelis subsector in the Inner Reach's Aurel sector that served as the throneworld of the both the Stellar Concord and the Stellar Dominion that replaced it. Prior to being covered by over five thousand metallic levels of what would become known as Dominion City, Aurelis was the ancient homeworld of the Tavren and Zhiri species. Though debated by historians, the theory that Aurelis was the ancestral world of humans remained unproven. Noted for its cosmopolitan culture and towering skyscrapers, Aurelis's population consisted of trillions of citizens hailing from a vast array of both humanoid and alien species. In addition, Aurelis's strategic location at the end of several major trade routes enabled it to grow in power and influence, causing the city-planet to surpass its early rivals and become the hub of galactic culture, education, finance, fine arts, politics and technology. It was the location of several major landmarks, including the Sentari Temple, Monument Plaza, and the Senate Building.

Serving as the capital of the galaxy since the ancient days of the old Stellar Concord, Aurelis was at the center of many historic events, such as the Sentari Order's conflict with the Vorrak. Although the planet fell under Vorrak control for a time, Concord forces ultimately liberated Aurelis with the aid of the Sentari Knights. Their victory was so decisive that at one point the Vorrak became all but extinct, replacing an age of darkness and strife with the democratic rule of the modern Stellar Concord. Retaining its status as galactic capital, it was on Aurelis where the Stellar Senate governed the galaxy for centuries, establishing an era of peace that lasted for almost a millennium under the protection of the Sentari peacekeepers. However, political intrigue wrought by corruption and secession transformed Aurelis into a dangerous environment by the time of the Replica Wars with the League of Free Worlds. On the eve of the New Order, fulfilling a grand scheme, Supreme Chancellor Corvin Drexmar, who was, in fact, the Vorrak Lord Xarn Morvath, declared the Sentari enemies of the state, resulting in a galaxy-wide purge that all but destroyed the Sentari Order. Without the Sentari to oppose him, Drexmar dissolved the Concord and declared himself Stellar Sovereign to the applause of the now Dominion Senate.

Aurelis continued to serve as galactic capital under the Dominion, having become the Sovereign's throneworld, although Drexmar himself disappeared from public view for the most part, leaving the day-to-day governing to his advisors in the Dominion Ruling Council. The Dominion maintained a firm hold on Aurelis throughout the Sovereign's reign, but with his defeat at the Battle of Sylvar in 4 ABO by the Alliance to Restore the Concord, citizens of Aurelis celebrated by revolting. Unable to command the military's allegiance as the insurgents restored the Concord, Grand Vizier Ves Ordrell's rule was limited to Aurelis while Fleet Admiral Ostin Varr, the self-proclaimed Counselor to the Dominion, assumed de facto control of the collapsing Dominion government. Hoping to escape prosecution for war crimes, Ordrell ultimately surrendered himself to the New Concord in return for amnesty. Following Varr's death at the Battle of Sarrow in 5 ABO, Ordrell signed the Stellar Concordance - a peace treaty recognizing the Dominion's capitulation and the end of the Stellar Civil War. Ordrell himself was permitted to return to Aurelis as the head of a provisional government. Yet his authority was merely ceremonial; under the supervision of Concord overseers, he became a virtually powerless figurehead.
