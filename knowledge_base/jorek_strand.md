# Jorek Strand

Jorek Strand was a human male crime lord and former bounty hunter whose career spanned decades, from the fall of the Stellar Concord to the end of the rule of the Stellar Dominion. Originally code-named Alpha, he was an unaltered replica of the famed Kethari bounty hunter, Varek Strand, who raised the boy as his son. Jorek emulated his father and genetic donor by wearing a customized suit of Kethari armor. His personal starship was the Iron Shackle, a Raptor-31-class patrol and attack craft that once belonged to Varek. Trained in combat and martial skills from a young age, Strand was one of the most feared bounty hunters in the galaxy during the reign of Sovereign Drexmar. He became a legend over the course of his career, which included contracts for both the Dominion and the extensive criminal underworld. After living among the Varrak Raiders of Drassa for a time, he took over as Overlord of Kel Varra during the era of the New Concord.

Jorek was created on the Untamed Space planet of Tidewell as a result of an arrangement between the Vorrak Lord Xarn Taloth and Varek Strand, a Kethari foundling who served as the template of the Grand Army of the Concord. Unlike the replica soldiers grown from Varek's DNA, Varek regarded Jorek as his son. In accordance with certain Kethari traditions, Jorek was also considered a foundling by virtue of his descent from Varek, whose chain code documented his participation in the Kethari Civil War and his ownership of the armor later inherited by Jorek. During the last days of the Secessionist Crisis, Jorek and Varek retreated to Ferrox where the latter was killed in battle by Sentari Master Daro Kesh. Orphaned by Varek's death, Jorek sought vengeance by attempting to assassinate Kesh, although his plan proved unsuccessful. After serving time in prison on Aurelis, Strand took up his late father's profession as a bounty hunter, working alongside other mercenaries, such as Grisk, Merrik, and Zara Quillon, while also notably apprenticing himself to his father's infamous brief protégée, Dov Rattigan.

In the aftermath of the Replica Wars, Strand continued to make a name for himself while working for the gangster Gorrba Velikor Thuun, as well as the Vorrak Lord Xarn Velgor. During the Stellar Civil War, the bounty hunter managed to track down Captain Dax Corvo to Nimbaro on a contract for the Stellar Dominion, hoping to deliver him to Gorrba's Palace on Drassa. During the journey, Strand stopped on the moon of Vell Shadda in order to make sure Corvo would survive his time in stasisite, but he lost the smuggler while he was away as part of the return of the Scarlet Dusk, kicking off the War of the Bounty Hunters. Ultimately, Strand regained Corvo and delivered him to the impatient Gorrba.
