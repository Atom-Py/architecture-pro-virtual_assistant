# Feron Maddox

Feronis Baltren Maddox, known as Feron Maddox, was a human male smuggler, gambler and the baron administrator of Sky Haven, a floating mining colony in the upper atmosphere of the gas planet Nimbaro. Before settling on Nimbaro, Maddox owned the Eternal Talon and filled it with modifications for comfort and luxury, but he lost the ship to his friend and rival Dax Corvo in a game of trivex.

When Dax Corvo, Princess Lyssa Varella and Grawrok sought refuge on Sky Haven, Maddox betrayed them to Xarn Velgor after the Dominion threatened his city. He soon turned against the Dominion, helped his friends escape and joined the Free Alliance. Together with Grawrok he rescued Corvo from Gorrba's Palace on Drassa. In 4 ABO, as a general of the Alliance, Maddox flew the Eternal Talon at the Battle of Sylvar and helped destroy the second Void Core from within.
