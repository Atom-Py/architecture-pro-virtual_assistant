# Sylvar

Sylvar, designated KV7718-C, also known as the Forest Moon of Sylvar or the Haven Moon, was a small forested moon that was the ninth moon that orbited the Far Fringe planet of the same name. It was the homeworld of the sentient Drogg, Tikku, and Fezzal species, as well as the Glimmet and semi-sentient Hrovak races.

A remote moon in the Sylvar system of the Far Fringe Territories, Sylvar was covered in dense woodlands, tall mountains, and savannas, and home to the sentient Tikku, Drogg, and Fezzal species. The semi-sentient Hrovak also dwelled on the moon as well as the Glimmet. Some of Sylvar's trees reached more than 1,000 meters in height. It contained a breathable atmosphere to humans and 8% surface water. It also had two suns: Sylvar I and Sylvar II.

Throughout galactic history, offworld raiders occasionally came to Sylvar to hunt the native Tikku population to make Tikku Jerky, which was a popular snack across the Far Fringe.

During the reign of the Stellar Dominion, the Dominion, deeming Sylvar to be perfect for its purposes due to how remote the moon was, established an outpost on the moon that included a shield generator complex to protect the incomplete second Void Core in orbit of the moon. The Dominion occupied Sylvar. In order to make way for the complex, a Tikku village was razed by Dominion troops, leading to strained Dominion - Tikku relations. As a result of the grav-lift field emitted by the SLD-26 planetary shield generator to keep the Void Core in a stationary orbit, Sylvar was prone to earthquakes, tidal imbalances, and other geological disturbances that slowly but surely began to break up the small moon. The Dominion also established other facilities beyond the shield generator complex, including Platform 4, another bunker, and Research Station 9, which was established by the Vosk Initiative.

The moon was later the site of the monumental Battle of Sylvar that saw combat between the Alliance to Restore the Concord and its Tikku allies against Dominion forces who were attempting to protect the shield generator and prevent the Alliance from destroying its superweapon in orbit. After fierce combat on the forest moon, the Free Alliance managed not only to destroy the shield generator complex, but also the second Void Core. With the destruction of the Dominion's dreaded battle station and the death of Sovereign Drexmar and his enforcer, Xarn Velgor, the insurgents and Tikkus held a victory celebration on the moon, one of many across the galaxy.

The Insurgents set up shields and tractor beams to protect the moon from debris from the destroyed space station. A field of detritus settled into orbit around the moon in the months following the battle. Additionally, the many hyperspace anomalies present in the Saddren sector absorbed the worst of the Void Core debris fallout. Despite that, some debris still rained down on the moon for a decade after the battle, but Sylvar's ecosystem proved resilient.
