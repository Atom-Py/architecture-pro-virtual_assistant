# Kryos

Kryos was a remote, icy planet that was the sixth planet in the star system of the same name, located in the Tarnis sector of the galaxy's Far Fringe Territories. It was the homeworld to the primitive frostmaw species.

It notably hosted Frost Base during the Stellar Civil War, the temporary headquarters of the Alliance to Restore the Concord, until the Stellar Dominion located them, initiating a major confrontation known as the Battle of Kryos. In the months following, scavengers established temporary base camps on the planet to scrap the MT-HWs destroyed.

In the months after the Battle of Sylvar, rumors emerged about the New Concord intending to repurpose Kryos into a prison for those who remained loyal to the Dominion, however, the credibility of the rumors were dismissed as Chancellor Sera Valcourt had been dedicated to a pretext of justice over punishment. Meanwhile, the war returned to Kryos, where it became the site of another battle during the Iron Blockade.

From space, the planet of Kryos looked like a pale blue orb due to its dense snow and ice covering. Five planets existed between Kryos and its sun, while an asteroid belt surrounded the planet with meteors occasionally striking the surface. Most of its surface was covered with glaciers and frozen ice plains. Despite the characterization of Kryos as being a planet of frozen tundras and nothing more, a characterization the naturalist Yen Morosh said would be fair if someone only viewed the world from orbit, another third of the planet was covered by oceans.

The temperature, although always frigid, was known to drop to -60°C come nightfall. Although devoid of intelligent life, Kryos was home to fifteen subspecies of snowrunners, which were commonly used by Insurgents as a means of transport, and which Dax Corvo cut open to use as shelter to keep Taren Solvane and himself warm during a snowstorm. It was also home to a species of towering predators known as frostmaws. During his stay on Kryos, Solvane was captured by a frostmaw and was hung upside down in the frostmaw's cave, waiting to be eaten. Using the power of the Synth Flux, Solvane managed to get his hands on his lumen blade and cut himself down from the roof and sever the frostmaw's right arm. The planet also had a number of "crystalline geysers."

By the time of the Stellar Civil War between the Stellar Dominion and the Alliance to Restore the Concord, Kryos was undergoing an ice age. Various smuggling rings tried to make use of Kryos as a base, yet their efforts always failed due to the planet's extreme conditions. Sometime following the Battle of Orrin, Velasko's Gang, itself an insurgent cell, established a base on Kryos. Kryos was first surveyed for use as an insurgent base by Corporal Kettleby. Hal Brennick, a general in the Alliance to Restore the Concord, listed Kryos among his preliminary scans for possible Alliance bases. With Kryos a world so obscure it was not on most star maps despite being extremely close to the Brannickan Trade Spine, it looked to be a perfect spot for an insurgent base. Indeed, its surface was a winter wasteland, Kryos sat on a lesser used hyperlane, and the asteroid field around Kryos provided an extra degree of protection. That field hampered travel through the Kryos system, and any asteroid crash into the surface would create enough of a thermal signature that it hid signs of intelligent life.
