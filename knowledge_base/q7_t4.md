# Q7-T4

Q7-T4, pronounced Queseven-Teefour and often referred to as Q7 (Queseven), was a Q7-series navigator automaton manufactured by Mechanica Works with masculine programming. A smart, spunky automaton who served a multitude of leaders, Q7-T4 was never given a full memory wipe nor did he ever receive new programming, with these factors resulting in an adventurous and independent attitude. Often finding himself in pivotal moments in galactic history, his bravery and ingenuity saved the galaxy on numerous occasions.

Beginning his service in the employ of Queen Avarre of Velora, Q7-T4 wound up serving Sentari Knight Kael Solvane during the waning years of the Stellar Concord, often accompanied by the protocol automaton K-5LO in many adventures throughout the Replica Wars. After Solvane turned to the shadow current of the Synth Flux, the automaton served Senator Edran Varella for a time in the Dominion Senate. Around nineteen years following the purge of the Stellar Concord, Q7-T4 played a pivotal role in helping the Free Alliance destroy the Dominion's Void Core superweapon. He carried technical readouts vital to its destruction. Serving Sentari Knight Taren Solvane, the son of his two former masters throughout the Stellar Civil War, he participated in both the Battles of Kryos and Sylvar, and witnessed the successful destruction of the Dominion's second Void Core four years after the first one was demolished.

In the subsequent thirty years, Q7 accompanied Taren Solvane until the undoing of his attempted resurrection of the Sentari Order in 28 ABO. Following Taren's disappearance, Q7 entered a self-imposed low power mode as newer automaton models started to out-compute the aging navigator. Despite this, his celebrated role in the Uprising protected him from the usual recycling procedure of many old automatons, allowing him a semi-retirement in the Vanguard as he pored over several decades of uninterrupted data, and "dreamed" of his greatest adventures.

Following his reawakening after the Battle of Sunbreaker Base, Q7 went on to accompany the flux-sensitive scavenger Nyra on her journey to train under Taren Solvane on Ilun-Vey, the planet of the First Sentari Temple, where the automaton was reunited with his old master. Q7-T4 continued to aid the Vanguard alongside his longtime counterpart K-5LO throughout the Iron Mandate-Vanguard War, culminating in a decisive victory at the Battle of Nocthar, in which he was an active participant in the role of copilot for General Kip Aldane.

Q7-T4 was built around prior or during 32 BBO. He was one of several navigator automatons on Queen Selene Avarre's Velora Royal Starship during the invasion of Velora by the Merchant Combine that year. The ship was used by Queen Avarre and her entourage in an attempt to pass the blockade and escape the planet. Unluckily for the passengers, the vessel took a hit to the shield generator from a Merchant Combine battleship. The four navigators were sent out to repair the ship, but only Q7-T4 survived - the rest were pulverized by stray pulse gun fire. Q7-T4 was able to bring the shields online by bypassing the main power drive, allowing the ship to narrowly escape into hyperspace.
