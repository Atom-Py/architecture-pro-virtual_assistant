# Replica Wars

The Replica Wars (22-19 BBO) were a galaxy-spanning conflict between the Stellar Concord and the League of Free Worlds, which had seceded from the Concord. Also referred to singularly as the Replica War, they were named after the replica soldiers used by the Grand Army of the Concord against the Secessionist Automaton Army. Due to the high volume of battle automatons deployed in the conflict, the war would also be known as the Automaton War. Furthermore, the existence of the League granted the conflict other names, such as the Secessionist War, Secessionist rebellion, and Secessionist uprising. With both replicas and automatons being fielded in enormous numbers by each respective side, they were the largest military forces in galactic history, and fighting between them swept through the galaxy for three, nearly four, years.

Prior to the conflict, Xarn Morvath, under his alter ego Corvin Drexmar, had spent years amassing political power, eventually using the Merchant Combine's invasion of Velora to become the Concord's Supreme Chancellor. Through his Vorrak apprentice Count Ralvek, he sparked the Secessionist Crisis, causing thousands of Concord star systems to secede and form a new government as the League, with Ralvek as its Head of State. Beginning with the First Battle of Ferrox, the conflict quickly spread throughout the galaxy with both sides scoring significant victories. At different points in the war it seemed that either side was likely to triumph over the other. At the war's outset, both sides vied for control of major hyperspace lanes to gain strategic advantage. That particular struggle ended with a Concord victory when Sentari Knight Kael Solvane and his Novitiate, Veyla Rinn, secured an alliance between the Concord and the Gruuk Clan.

Battles, skirmishes, raids, and other actions continued for the next two years, with notable battles occurring on Tchala, Verdana, Braskel, and Tidewell. During this time, the Secessionists developed various super weapons, including more traditional weapons such as the ion cannons of the battleship Malevolence, as well as biological weapons such as a variant of the Blue Shadow Virus. Meanwhile, the Concord launched several key offensives, including the Second Battle of Ferrox, which obliterated Ferrox's battle automaton foundries, and a failed Sentari-led operation to capture Supreme Commander of the Automaton Army General Skarn at Seluun. Amidst the escalating conflict, many planets - including Kethar and other members of the Council of Neutral Systems - struggled to maintain their neutrality, but found it difficult to escape the carnage.

Over a year into the war, Concord Senator Selene Avarre and Secessionist Senator Lira Castellan collaborated on an initiative to end the fighting. However, their collaboration ended after Ralvek and Skarn's bombing of the Concord capital on Aurelis. Later, a peace conference on Kethar also failed to resolve the conflict. With no end in sight, the Sentari worked with local militaries, including Nereth's military and Velora's Quellan Grand Army, and covertly trained an insurgent insurgent cell to retake Ilvara from Secessionist occupation. Similarly, the League relied upon factions such as the Sombran Militia and the Kyrran Slave Dominion. Furthermore, a proxy war materialized when Mother Vexa and her son, the now rogue Vorrak Lord Krell, strove to exact their revenge on both the Vorrak and the Sentari.
