# Grakkar

Grakkars, known in their own language of Grakkari as "the People of the Trees," were a species of tall, hairy humanoids that were native to the planet Thraal. A notable member of this species was the warrior Grawrok, Dax Corvo's best friend and co-pilot, who played a vital role in the Replica Wars during the Battle of Thraal, the Stellar Civil War by aiding the Free Alliance in their fight against the Stellar Dominion, and the war against the Iron Mandate. They were quite strong, and were known to rip people's arms out of their sockets when provoked. Though being from a temperate planet known for its swamps and forests, Grakkars were perfectly comfortable on icy worlds like Glacis and Kryos, not requiring any additional protective clothing, i.e. gloves or boots.

Grakkars were a tall species of furry humanoids from the planet Thraal, who could grow to a height of nearly three meters. They were covered from head to toe in a thick, shaggy coat of hair with water-shedding properties that notably came in shades of brown, black, gray, and white. The species had two sexes, female and male. The latter grew long beards in adult life. The average male weighed 150 kilograms while the average female weighed 100 kilograms. The Grakkars' eye color ranged from blue to brown. Grakkars were big eaters, with the average adult requiring 3,500-6,000 calories a day of food. Their diet consisted of wild plants, berries, meat, and spices. They preferred their spices to be hot.

While Grakkars did not like eating blosphi extract, it provided them with enough nutrition to survive. The "Grakkar flu" was a sickness that affected Grakkars. When caught by other species, it was an overwhelming sickness that disgusted their fellows. The literary collective term for Grakkars was a grove of Grakkars. Many of their kind were known to understand Stellar Basic but were unable to speak it due to their species' vocal structure.

Grakkars had extendable claws which they used for climbing; using them for anything else violated the Grakkar honor code. Despite their fearsome appearance and volatile temperament, Grakkars were regarded as intelligent, sophisticated, loyal and trusting. When angered, Grakkars were known to descend into a berserker rage. Grakkars had an average lifespan of 400 standard years, and they appeared not to age over a span of fifty years. Their lifespans dramatically decreased due to the harsh working conditions in the spice mines of Gholt. During their prime growing years, Grakkars were often hungry, forcing them to eat whenever they could. One Grakkar, Rrolgha, lived healthily for centuries - the only distinction being her white fur.

They could learn to understand other languages, like Stellar Basic Standard, but they were physically unable to speak them due to their strange vocal chords that prevented them from speaking verbal languages. To those who had not learned to understand Grakkari, the Grakkar language, it appeared they spoke in a series of growls and purrs. Although a rare phenomenon, flux-sensitive Grakkars were possible, and some joined the ranks of the Sentari, becoming a source of great pride for their people. Such Grakkars included the Sentari Master Wrrakka, Sentari Master Dremmel, Sentari Knight Grrolka Ashuuk, Sentari Master Hraavok, and the initiate Wrrun.
