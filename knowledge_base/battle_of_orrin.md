# Battle of Orrin

The Battle of Orrin, also known as the Battle of the Void Core or the Miracle of Orrin, was a major and decisive battle of the Stellar Civil War, in which the Free Alliance was able to destroy the Void Core and was seen as one of the first major victories over the Stellar Dominion.

During the Battle of Pelagos, Ilsa Reyl and Rafe Callen managed to steal detailed plans and design specifications for the Dominion battle station known as the Void Core and transmit them to the Alliance Fleet above; these plans soon made their way to Princess Lyssa Varella. Princess Lyssa was able to place the Void Core plans within Q7-T4. Shortly after Dominion forces under the command of the Dark Lord of the Vorrak, Xarn Velgor, soon captured Princess Lyssa and the crew of the Verity IV.

Princess Lyssa was taken prisoner aboard the Void Core by Lord Velgor to High Warden Aldric Vosk. The Princess was rescued from captivity by an unlikely alliance of the smuggler Dax Corvo, former Sentari Master Ivo-Ren Malden, a young farmboy from Drassa named Taren Solvane, and Corvo's First Mate, the Grakkar named Grawrok. The group, which also included Free Alliance automatons Q7-T4 and K-5LO, in possession of the Void Core plans, fled to the Insurgent base on Orrin 4 - closely pursued by Vosk and the battle station.

Before Varella arrived, Chancellor Sera Valcourt was evacuated aboard a shuttle, as General Brennick considered her the core of the rebellion. During her time away, she composed a contingency plan: surrender to Sovereign Drexmar.

General Hal Brennick briefed the insurgent pilots on the plan. The Void Core's defenses were designed for a direct, large-scale assault. By using small, one-man starfighters, the Insurgents would slip through the Void Core's main defenses, maneuver down a narrow trench leading to the exhaust port, and fire proton torpedoes into the exhaust port. An analysis of the plans identified a weakness in the battle station, placed there by Dominion scientist Tomas Reyl: a small thermal exhaust port leading to the main reactor. A proton torpedo fired into the port would cause a chain reaction that would destroy the battle station.

With the Void Core approaching, H-blades from Amber Wing and Z-blades from Crimson Wing and Jade Wing were dispatched to carry out the attack plan. A total of 30 ships were deployed in the assault on the Void Core: 8 H-blades from Amber Wing as well as 22 Z-blades, 12 from Crimson Wing and the other 10 from Jade Wing. The starfighters weaved through the turret emplacements, destroying many as they passed. Crimson Six (Bram Tollo Hensk) was the first to be destroyed in the volley of turret fire. The deployment of Dominion TVX fighters, including one piloted by Lieutenant Kira Delmont, devastated the Insurgent attackers, destroying them far faster and more efficiently than the station's weapon emplacements had been able to. Crimson Four (Ned T. Halloway) was the first to be shot down by the incoming TVXs.
