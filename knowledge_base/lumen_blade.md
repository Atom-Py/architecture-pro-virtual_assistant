# Lumen blade

The lumen blade, also referred to as a laser sword by those who were unfamiliar with it, was a weapon usually used by the Sentari, Vorrak, and other flux-sensitives. Lumen blades had a plasma blade emitted from a usually metal hilt, and could be shut off at will or at the touch of a button. The hilt contained a power cell, like the Vorinium power cell in Xarn Velgor's lumen blade. It also contained a vael crystal which had been attuned to the Synth Flux by a Sentari, and which amplified the energy from the power cell to create the plasma beam, as well as containing it within a blade-like field. The energy blade had no mass, but lumen blades operated on principles of controlled electromagnetic arc-wave energy, creating a gyroscopic effect that made them challenging to handle. It was a weapon that required skill and training, and was greatly enhanced when used in conjunction with the Synth Flux. Though also used by the Vorrak, the lumen blade was synonymous with the Sentari, with some in the galaxy believing only Sentari could use lumen blades.

Lumen blades were generally used for both offense and defense. A lumen blade could cut through virtually anything, from flesh to blast doors. The only ways to block the incoming attack of a lumen blade was with a weapon made with material that conducted energy, such as a shockstaff and a R8 riot control baton, resistant metals like ostrium and nullium, another lumen blade or an ichor sword. When used defensively, a flux-sensitive could deflect pulse gun bolts with a lumen blade, and with skill, could even reflect the shots back toward the shooter or some other target. Experienced Sentari could even employ their lumen blades to absorb Synth Flux lightning. Most practitioners used one single-bladed lumen blade, though some used double-bladed lumen blades or even multiple lumen blades at once. Despite lacking flux-sensitivity, the cyborg General Skarn was capable of wielding four lumen blades at once thanks to his advanced mechanical body and enhanced cybernetic brain.

Lumen blade combat was divided into seven Forms: Form I, Form II, Form III, Form IV, Form V, Form VI, and Form VII.

Sounding like no other weapon in the galaxy, lumen blades were used thousands of years before the age of the Stellar Concord, seeing use in the Great Scourge of Vharlos. They were also used in the Battles of Dravenmoor and the Peacekeeping of Dunmerrow, The proto-blade was an ancient type of prototype lumen blade.

During the history of the Sentari Order, the lumen blade was considered to be the Sentari's only true ally in battle. As Master Ivo-Ren Malden once declared to his Novitiate Kael Solvane in the days of the late Concord, the lumen blade was a Sentari's life. Sentari saw the lumen blade as a symbol of the care that they would wield using their gifts given by the Synth Flux, and also served as a message to their opponents: mastering the lumen blade required great skill and training. The choice of the saber was also the symbol of their role and duty as protectors, not destroyers, and the fact that the Sentari did not seek power and only sought to stand tall against darkness. Still, Sentari knew they would need to be more than their lumen blades.
