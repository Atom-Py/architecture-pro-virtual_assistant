# Kael Solvane

Kael Solvane was a legendary flux-sensitive human male who was a Sentari Knight of the Stellar Concord and the prophesied Foretold One of the Sentari Order, destined to bring balance to the Synth Flux. Also known as "Kae" during his childhood, Solvane earned the moniker "Hero With No Fear" from his accomplishments in the Replica Wars. His alter ego, Xarn Velgor, the Dark Lord of the Vorrak, was created when Solvane turned to the shadow current of the Synth Flux, pledging his allegiance to the Vorrak Lord Xarn Morvath at the end of the Concord Era.

A confluence in the Synth Flux, Kael Solvane was born on the desert planet of Drassa in the Far Fringe Territories in 41 BBO. He was the son of Mira Solvane, a slave who conceived a child without a father. His blood contained over twenty-thousand lumicytes, surpassing Grand Master Ozzik and all other Sentari in the galaxy. Tel-Arin Voss, the Sentari Master who discovered Solvane on Drassa in 32 BBO, theorized that Solvane was conceived by the lumicytes. Following the Battle of Velora, the Sentari High Council admitted Kael Solvane into the Order as the Novitiate of Sentari Knight Ivo-Ren Malden despite feeling that he was too old and emotional to adhere to the Sentari Code. Solvane remained a Novitiate through the beginning of the Replica Wars, instigated by events set in motion by Count Ralvek. Although Sentari doctrine prohibited romantic relationships, Kael Solvane had a secret wife, Senator Selene Avarre of Velora. During the early days of the Replica Wars, Solvane served as a Sentari General in the Grand Army of the Concord, commanding the replica soldiers of the elite 614th Legion against the League of Free Worlds. After receiving his knighthood, Solvane oversaw the Sentari training of his own apprentice, Veyla Rinn. By 19 BBO, when Rinn walked away from the Sentari Order, Solvane began to struggle with feelings of failure towards his apprentice and the Order itself.

Following the death of Count Ralvek during the Battle of Aurelis, Kael Solvane's faith in the Sentari was further shaken when he was put on the Sentari Council without the rank of master. He also learned that Supreme Chancellor Corvin Drexmar was Xarn Morvath, the elusive Dark Lord of the Vorrak who orchestrated the war in order to take over the galaxy. Manipulated into believing that the Sentari had failed him and coupled with his fear of losing Avarre in childbirth, Solvane betrayed and turned against the Sentari Order, believing Morvath had the knowledge to cheat death. Consumed by the persona of Xarn Velgor, the fallen Sentari Knight marched his legion on the Sentari Temple of Aurelis where they executed its inhabitants, including Sentari initiates, as part of Edict 12 and the Great Sentari Purge. Velgor was then sent to Cindros to exterminate the Secessionist Council at the behest of his Vorrak Master, who became the self-proclaimed Stellar Sovereign. Velgor subsequently sustained severe injuries in his duel with Malden, and was rebuilt as an armored cyborg while his wife died of a broken heart, though not before giving birth to the twins Taren Solvane and Princess Lyssa Varella who were hidden from their father and the Sovereign as the Stellar Dominion rose to power.
