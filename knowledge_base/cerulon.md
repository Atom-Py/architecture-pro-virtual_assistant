# Cerulon

Cerulon, located in the Inner Reach, was a terrestrial planet covered with mountains. During the waning decades of the Stellar Concord, it was ruled by Queen Ysolde Varella and represented in the Stellar Senate by her husband, Senator Edran Calder Varella. Cerulon was regarded for its natural beauty, its tranquility, and the sophistication of its arts and culture.

Following the rise of Corvin Drexmar's Stellar Dominion, Cerulon played a pivotal role in the establishment of the Free Alliance, a movement that sought to restore the Old Concord's values, and was one of its earliest supporters. Because of its ties with the Alliance, the planet was destroyed when High Warden Aldric Vosk decided to test the superlaser of the Void Core, a moon-sized battle station developed by the Dominion, at full strength. Among the few survivors of that disaster was Princess Lyssa Varella, adopted daughter of Edran and Ysolde Varella and heiress to the royal house of Cerulon.

From space, the planet of Cerulon, located in the star system of the same name within the galaxy's Inner Reach, appeared as a blue-green orb enveloped in a white web of clouds. Its surface had vast bodies of water and was covered in snow-capped mountains, with patches of green grassy hills. Cerulon was known to the galaxy as "the planet of beauty." Cerulonians were very artistic and lived for this beauty, but the planet still hid treacherous cliffs and forgotten crevasses. Famous natural landmarks of Cerulon included the Cloudshape Falls, the Serrin Lagoons, and the Esmareth rain forest.

A variety of grapevines grew on Cerulon, which notably allowed to produce Teneray and emerald wine. The local fauna included the Cerulonian wolf-cat, woolhorns, and a species of white-winged bird.

The cities on Cerulon were carefully designed to respect the natural beauty of their environment. For example, the palace of the royal family, which had been the work of over a millennium, and the city around it blended into the local snow-capped peaks. Cerulonian architecture made use of white synthstone, which inspired the architecture of certain mining economies of the Far Fringe Territories, like the floating colony of Sky Haven, and the planets Brenna and Ostrel.

Despite the lyrics of the traditional Cerulonian lullaby "Mirrorbright" referencing a moon, the planet never possessed any natural satellites.

The peaceful world of Cerulon was among the oldest members of the Stellar Concord, the democratic union that governed most of the galaxy, and was among the worlds that originally founded it. Upon the formation of its monarchy it was established that the heir apparent to Cerulon must complete a Challenge of the Heart, Challenge of the Mind and Challenge of the Body which would be announced on their Day of Demand and upon completion they would be officially invested as heir. In ancient times, Cerulonian heirs who partook in their Day of Demand had to fight their way into the palace throne room. Before they permanently settled on Aurelis, the Sentari High Council was known to have a council on Cerulon. Cerulon at one point had a neo-classical period, which produced unique architecture. The Eastwind Manor of House Calder was built in the neo-classical style.
