# Grawrok

Grawrok, known affectionately to his friends as Grawk, was a Grakkar male warrior, smuggler, mechanic, pilot, and resistance fighter who fought in the Replica Wars, the Stellar Civil War, the Cold War, and the subsequent Iron Mandate-Vanguard War. He hailed from the planet Thraal where he was born during the High Concord Era and became a Grakkar military leader. During the Replica Wars, he was captured by Skarrid slavers and held captive on Grrasha, but he worked with a fellow captive, Sentari Commander Veyla Rinn, to escape. He later commanded Grakkar forces of the Rrovash Grakkar Militia during the Battle of Thraal alongside the Grand Army of the Concord, led by Sentari General Ozzik. During the battle, one of the last ones of the war, Ozzik's replica soldiers received Edict 12 from the Vorrak Lord Xarn Morvath and, with the help of Grawrok and his fellow Grakkar Chieftain and General Grrabek, Ozzik escaped Thraal and the destruction of the Sentari Order.

In the years that followed, during the rule of the Stellar Dominion, Grawrok was enslaved by the Dominion until he was rescued by Corporal Dax Corvo, becoming his co-pilot aboard the Eternal Talon during a raid on Gholt for Scarlet Dusk with Elias Crane and Lin'ra. Winning the Talon from Feronis Baltren Maddox, they later worked in the employ of crime lord Gorrba the Gruuk until Corvo was forced to dump a shipment of spice to avoid trouble with the Dominion, at which point a bounty was placed on their heads. Soon thereafter, Grawrok and Corvo were hired by Sentari Master Ivo-Ren Malden to ferry him, Taren Solvane, K-5LO, and Q7-T4 to Cerulon on a mission vital to the survival of the Alliance to Restore the Concord. After finding Cerulon was destroyed, the group was brought aboard the Void Core, where they helped Princess Lyssa Varella escape from captivity and return to the Insurgent base on Orrin 4. Though Grawrok and Corvo initially chose to leave Orrin 4 and not join the Uprising, they ended up fighting in the Battle of Orrin and were instrumental in Solvane's destruction of the Void Core, a major victory for the Uprising.

Throughout the war, Grawrok and Corvo fought the Dominion in a number of engagements, including an assault on Ferrim 1 and the battle on Tolvar Ves. Along with Princess Varella and K-5LO, they escaped from Frost Base on the planet Kryos after the Dominion tracked the Free Alliance there. The group sought refuge with Maddox on Sky Haven, but Maddox betrayed them and turned them over to Xarn Velgor, the Sovereign's chief enforcer, after his city was threatened. Corvo was frozen in stasisite and Maddox helped the others escape, but they were too late to save the frozen Corvo from being taken by Jorek Strand to Gorrba the Gruuk. Grawrok and Maddox set off to find Corvo and located him in Gorrba's Palace on Drassa, where Grawrok and his friends saved Corvo and destroyed Gorrba. Shortly thereafter, they fought in the Battle of Sylvar, where Grawrok helped destroy the shield generator and allowed the Alliance Fleet to destroy the VC-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station. Morvath was killed for the first time aboard the battle station, delivering a crippling blow to the Dominion.
