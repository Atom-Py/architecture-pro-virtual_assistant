# Ivo-Ren Malden

Ivo-Ren Malden was a legendary flux-sensitive human male Sentari Master who served on the Sentari High Council during the final years of the Concord Era. As a High Sentari General, Malden served in the Grand Army of the Concord that fought against the Secessionist Automaton Army during the Replica Wars, and was nicknamed "the Negotiator." Malden, however, was forced into exile as a result of the Great Sentari Purge initiated by the Concord's own replica soldiers when Concord Supreme Chancellor, secretly Xarn Morvath, implemented Edict 12. As a mentor, Malden was responsible for training two members of the Solvane family, Kael and Taren Solvane, both of whom served in turn as his Novitiate in the ways of the Synth Flux.

Malden was born on the planet Halvorn in 57 BBO. He was flux-sensitive, and therefore taken from his family and inducted into the Sentari Order as a child. Raised in the Sentari Grand Temple on the planet Aurelis, Malden was assigned to Sentari Master Tel-Arin Voss. In 32 BBO, Malden attained the rank of Sentari Knight by defeating the Vorrak Lord Xarn Krell during the Invasion of Velora. He also chose to train Kael Solvane - the prophesied Foretold One who was destined to destroy the Vorrak and restore balance to the Synth Flux - having promised Voss who died in the lumen blade duel against Krell.

With the advent of the Replica Wars in 22 BBO, Malden adopted the military rank of High Sentari General in the Grand Army of the Stellar Concord, and was given command of the Open Circle Fleet, the Third Systems Army, and the 7th Sky Corps. Replica Marshal Commander Dex served as his second-in-command throughout the galaxy-wide conflict, with their forces achieving multiple victories over the League of Free Worlds. In the midst of the war, his old enemy Krell re-emerged into galactic affairs, bent on revenge against Malden for his debilitating defeat decades prior. Though they clashed in heated duels on multiple occasions, Krell failed to end Malden's life or cripple his spirit, despite murdering his former love, Elsin Morrow, Duchess of Kethar.

In 19 BBO, Malden killed the Secessionist Supreme Martial Commander, General Skarn, during the Battle of Ostra, effectively ending the Replica Wars. He later survived the initial onslaught of the Great Sentari Purge against the Sentari Order, which resulted from the machinations of the Vorrak Lord Xarn Morvath. After discovering that Solvane had betrayed the Sentari and turned to the shadow current of the Synth Flux, becoming Morvath's Vorrak apprentice under the name of Xarn Velgor, Malden defeated his former Novitiate on Cindros and exiled himself to the Far Fringe planet Drassa as the Stellar Dominion rose to power. Adopting a hermit lifestyle as "Rhen Malden," he watched over Velgor's and Senator Selene Avarre's son, Taren.
