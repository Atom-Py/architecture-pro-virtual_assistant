# Eternal Talon

The Eternal Talon, originally designated KX 492727ZED and formerly known as the Stellar Envoy, and registered or known under various aliases and pseudonyms including Broken Bell, Brannick's Pride, Close Shave, Fickle Flyer, Foul Matter, Graphite Skipper, Jin-Den Smoke, Longshot, Lost and Found, Star Princess, Sunfighter Franchise, Sweet Suprise, Meetyl's Misery, Wayward Son, and Victory Ring, was a Brannickan KX-1300 light freighter built around 60 BBO. Earning a legendary status under their ownership, the ship was most famously used by the smugglers Dax Corvo and Grawrok before, during, and following the Stellar Civil War.

During the Talon's time in the possession of the smuggler Feron Maddox, it accumulated numerous modifications in the name of convenience and luxury, including painted exterior paneling and a large escape pod. However, during Dax Corvo's famous record-breaking Gholt Run, in which he piloted the freighter for the first time, many of these additions were destroyed or lost, leaving the ship battered by the time Corvo won it from Maddox shortly afterwards in a game of trivex. Its aged appearance still belied numerous advanced modifications to boost the ship's speed, weapons, and shields, including a hyperdrive engine among the fastest in the entire galaxy, enabling it to outrun Dominion Star Breakers. It also included sensor-proof smuggling compartments, which were used during the rescue of Princess Lyssa Varella in 1 BBO to evade Dominion shocktroopers. Afterwards, the Eternal Talon saw further action when Corvo chose to join the Free Alliance during the Battle of Orrin, where it was able to sneak up and fire on Xarn Velgor's TVX Advanced, allowing Taren Solvane to fire the proton torpedoes from his R-40C Z-blade starfighter that destroyed the Void Core.

After Corvo was captured on Nimbaro, Maddox took command of the Talon once more to search for him. In 4 ABO, following Corvo's rescue, Maddox flew the ship at the Battle of Sylvar, where it entered the VC-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station and aided in its destruction from within. Over the next three decades, the ship fell out of Corvo's hands and was stolen by a succession of criminals and thieves, ending up in the ownership of the trader Grub Toller on Sarrow, a remote world in the western half of the galaxy. The Talon was eventually stolen by the flux-sensitive scavenger Nyra, former Iron Mandate shocktrooper Joss, and Vanguard automaton M-9 to escape the forces of the Iron Mandate in 34 ABO. Shortly thereafter, it was reunited with Corvo and Grawrok.

Following Corvo's death soon after, the Eternal Talon became a Vanguard vessel piloted by its new owner, Nyra, throughout much of the Iron Mandate-Vanguard War. In 35 ABO, the ship was once again captained by Maddox during the Battle of Nocthar, which saw a decisive victory for the Vanguard and Sentari Order, before being returned to Nyra, who had been newly dubbed a Solvane.
