# Battle of Sylvar

The Battle of Sylvar, also known as the Mission to Sylvar, the assault on the second Void Core, or the attack on the second Void Core, was a major and decisive battle of the Stellar Civil War, one in which the Alliance to Restore the Concord shattered the rule of the Stellar Dominion and saw the destruction of the VC-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station and the deaths of Xarn Morvath and Xarn Velgor. The triumph of the Alliance over the Dominion, and the void left in the wake of the Sovereign's demise, gave the Alliance the opportunity to give rise to the New Concord, which ultimately prevailed over the Dominion in the Battle of Sarrow one year later. The legacy of the Battle of Sylvar reverberated for decades after the fighting ended, including into the brewing war between the Dominion-inspired Iron Mandate and the Vanguard that arose to combat it.

The origins of the Battle of Sylvar emerged from the discovery that the Dominion was using the Forest Moon of Sylvar as a staging ground to construct a second Void Core, the first of which had been destroyed four years earlier during the Battle of Orrin. Brovan spies employed by the Alliance gained the intelligence needed to determine the Void Core's location and learn that the Sovereign planned to personally oversee the final phases of construction, though the Alliance was unaware that this was an elaborate plot to destroy Taren Solvane, a Sentari and the son of Xarn Velgor, and the Alliance itself once and for all. The battle began after the Alliance Fleet assembled near Cindar, and special operations ground forces were deployed to Sylvar to disable the shield protecting the armored battle station.

The Alliance Fleet soon arrived to discover the Dominion Navy waiting for them, along with a fully operational Void Core. Allied with the native Tikkus, ground forces under the command of General Dax Corvo and Princess Lyssa Varella destroyed the shield, allowing the Alliance Fleet to attack the superweapon. Meanwhile, Solvane engaged Velgor in a final lumen blade duel aboard the Void Core. After defeating his father, the Sovereign betrayed Velgor by attempting to convince the Sentari Knight to kill Velgor and turn to the shadow current of the Synth Flux, only for Taren to refuse. To save his son from the wrath of the Sovereign, Velgor surprised and betrayed Morvath, killing the Dark Lord of the Vorrak, and sacrificing his own life in the process - an act that fulfilled the Sentari prophecy of the Foretold One and brought balance to the Synth Flux. Shortly thereafter, the Free Alliance forces destroyed the Void Core. Dominion forces were left in tatters, enabling the Alliance to form the New Concord and forge a new future free of the Vorrak and the rule of the Dominion, thus allowing the Sentari Order to be restored.
