# Xarn Morvath

Xarn Morvath, born Corvin Drexmar and also simply known as the Sovereign, was a male human Dark Lord of the Vorrak and Sovereign of the Stellar Dominion, with his reign lasting from 19 BBO to 4 ABO. Rising to power in the Stellar Senate as the senator of Velora, the secretive Vorrak Lord cultivated two identities, Morvath and Drexmar, using both to further his political career and deceive his way into accomplishing his goal. He orchestrated the fall of the Stellar Concord and the Sentari Order through the Replica Wars, and then established his reign over the galaxy which lasted until his death at the Battle of Sylvar. However, the shadow current of the Synth Flux gave rise to powers which allowed him to escape death, seeking further immortality until the Sovereign was eventually permanently destroyed and killed during the Battle of Nocthar by Nyra, his granddaughter born from his Vat-Born replica son Corvel and his daughter-in-law Isveth.

Born on the Middle Fringe planet Velora in 84 BBO as the High Concord Era ended, Corvin Drexmar possessed a strong connection to the Synth Flux, and was secretly trained in the ways of the Vorrak by Xarn Pyrros with Drexmar taking Xarn Morvath as his Vorrak persona. He eventually betrayed and killed his Vorrak Master, in order to train his own Vorrak apprentice, Xarn Krell, for the Law of Pairs limited their ranks to two Vorrak Lords at any given time. The Invasion of Velora, a crisis orchestrated by Morvath, led to the Senate to elect Drexmar as Supreme Chancellor, although this came at the loss of Krell, who was defeated and presumed killed in the Battle of Velora. The Secessionist Crisis and the Replica Wars were masterminded by Morvath and his second apprentice, Xarn Taloth, in an effort to achieve galactic dominance. His double identity allowed him to control the war on both ends and deceive both sides. Following an attempt to arrest the chancellor, the Great Sentari Purge commenced with Morvath enacting Edict 12.

With the Sentari all but extinct, Morvath installed the New Order in the Concord's place, declaring himself Stellar Sovereign and bringing an end to the millennia of democracy. Shortly after betraying and causing the death of Taloth, he took on a new apprentice, having lured the Sentari Knight Kael Solvane to the shadow current and transforming him into Xarn Velgor. Their reign was virtually unchallenged for a generation until the formation of the Alliance to Restore the Concord. During the Stellar Civil War, the Sovereign sought to betray Velgor, by replacing him with the latter's son, Taren Solvane. However, Morvath's plan backfired when Velgor ultimately renounced the shadow current and killed Morvath to protect Taren. Thus, with the Vorrak's destruction, the Synth Flux was brought back into balance, fulfilling Kael's destiny as the prophesied Foretold One.
