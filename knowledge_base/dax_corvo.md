# Dax Corvo

Dax Corvo, known only as Dax until being given the surname Corvo, was a human male smuggler and hero of the Alliance to Restore the Concord. He rose from an impoverished childhood to become captain of the Eternal Talon, a leader in the Free Alliance and an instrumental figure in the Stellar Civil War during the reign of the Stellar Dominion. Although he did not believe in the Synth Flux controlling his destiny, Dax came to believe in the cause of galactic freedom, joining Sentari Taren Solvane and Princess Lyssa Varella in the fight for the Uprising. Following the war, Dax and Lyssa welcomed a son, Rhen Corvo, who became the dark knight Vael Drax after being corrupted by the shadow current of the Synth Flux. Although their relationship floundered, Lyssa hoped that Dax, as Rhen's father, could find a way to break the shadow current's hold on their son. Dax Corvo became legendary across the galaxy as both a smuggler and war hero of the Uprising.

As a young man fighting for survival on the mean streets of Brannick, Dax yearned to fly among the stars with dreams of becoming the galaxy's best pilot. With a fellow gutterling named Lin'ra, Dax lived as a street thief with Brannick's notorious Pale Grubs gang until an important deal with Lady Vesh went bad, causing Dax to flee Brannick. He was given the full name "Dax Corvo" by a Dominion recruitment officer while entering the Dominion Academy. He became known as Cadet 124-329 while serving as a Dominion cadet, and was eventually assigned to the 224th Dominion Armored Division. On Moorvane, Dax met the thief Elias Crane and wound up deserting the Dominion and joining his crew to steal unrefined lumerium from Gholt in order to pay a debt to Marek Dross, a kingpin with the Scarlet Dusk crime syndicate. By the end of this scheme, during which he reunited with Lin'ra, Dax was forced to kill his mentor Crane after a brutal double-cross. Dax also became rivals and close friends with fellow smuggler Feron Maddox and the Grakkar Grawrok, the latter who became his best friend. Maddox owned the Eternal Talon, a fast ship with which Dax made the Gholt Run in just eight jumps and won from Feron in a game of trivex.

With only Grawrok remaining at his side, as his trusted copilot who swore a life debt to the Brannickan smuggler, Dax Corvo embarked on a smuggling career under the employ of Gorrba the Gruuk, a notorious gangster and crime lord of Drassa. After ditching a shipment of spice to avoid trouble with the Dominion, Corvo owed a great deal of money to Gorrba, who then placed a bounty on the smuggler's head, as a result. His fortune seemed to have changed when Dax and Grawrok were hired to charter Taren Solvane, Ivo-Ren Malden, and the automatons Q7-T4 and K-5LO to Cerulon, avoiding Dominion shocktroopers who were searching for Taren's automatons. However, as they find Cerulon destroyed by the Dominion's new superweapon, the Void Core, Corvo became caught up in the rebellion against the Dominion, rescuing Princess Lyssa Varella, delivering the stolen Void Core plans to the hidden Insurgent base on Orrin 4, and saved Taren from the Vorrak Lord Xarn Velgor in the Battle of Orrin, allowing Solvane to destroy the Void Core. Corvo fought with the Uprising for a number of years afterward, taking part in numerous operations and battles against the Dominion.
