# Velora

Velora was a bountiful planet in the Vessick sector of the Middle Fringe, in the Trailing Reaches and close to the border of the Far Fringe Territories. It was home to the indigenous Quellan species and to a population of humans known as the Velora. Velora was pushed to the forefront of galactic politics as the birthplace of the Dark Lord of the Vorrak Corvin Drexmar, who served as its representative in the Senate of the Stellar Concord.

Under the guise of his Vorrak title Xarn Morvath, Drexmar secretly engineered an invasion of his peaceful homeworld by the Merchant Combine, a megacorporation in a bid for power. Ostensibly done in protest over proposed taxation of formerly free trade routes, the Combine's invasion led to an outpouring of support from the Senate for both Drexmar and the newly-elected Queen of Velora, Selene Avarre. Avarre led a successful resistance movement and removed the Combine from her world during the Battle of Velora. Drexmar subsequently rose to the office of the Supreme Chancellor of the Concord, with Avarre eventually taking his place as the planet's senatorial representative.

Velora would commit itself firmly to democracy in the intervening years, staying loyal to the Concord during the pan-galactic Replica Wars, which saw several military engagements against the League of Free Worlds come to the planet. By the time of the war's end, Drexmar had amassed considerable power and proclaimed himself the head of a new Stellar Dominion. Drexmar's reign would eventually be ended by the Alliance to Restore the Concord, an insurgent movement led by Avarre's children, Lyssa Varella and Taren Solvane.

Despite his origins on the planet, Drexmar did not care at all for his homeworld and, following his death, Velora was named a priority target for Operation: Ashfall, a protocol that the Vorrak Lord had put in place to destroy Dominion worlds should they fail to protect their Sovereign. Through the intervention of Varella and Queen Ilda Merrow, however, Velora was spared from ruin and went on to become a member of the successor state to the Alliance, the New Concord. Decades later, the planet would again fight against tyranny, rising up against the military junta of the Iron Mandate during its war with Varella's Vanguard.

Velora was a small pastoral world in the Middle Fringe, located near the border of the Far Fringe Territories, 34,000 light-years from the Inner Reach. Unlike most other worlds, the ancient planet lacked a molten core, and instead comprised a conglomerate of large, rocky bodies that made up a network of tunnels and flooded caves. The native Quellans, who built their homes in the waters of Velora, so deep that they went right through the planet, which they rarely ventured into the core, fearing the ravenous sea beasts which resided in areas such as the Caves of Ossarin. Despite this, certain Quellan navigators utilized time-honored trade routes through the planet's structure, which served as the most expedient avenues to reach other areas of the planet. Velora's interior was rich with plasma, a naturally-occurring energy unique to the planet, and this coupled with its lack of molten core gave Velora a reputation as an enigma to astrophysicists, who regarded the world's structure as an extremely rare phenomenon in the galaxy.
