# Vorrak

The Vorrak, also referred to as the Vorrak Order, was an ancient religious order of flux-wielders devoted to the shadow current of the Synth Flux. Driven by their raw emotions, including hate, anger, and greed, the Vorrak were deceptive and obsessed with gaining power no matter the cost. The order had many forms until it reached the apex of its power under Xarn Morvath, the Dark Lord of the Vorrak who achieved his order's goal of galactic conquest after a millennium of plotting. Within a generation, however, the deaths of Xarn Morvath and Xarn Velgor marked the end of the Vorrak Lords. Though the Vorrak ultimately went extinct with Morvath's demise, they still had a powerful legacy, and many continued to follow their teachings. There were also unfound ancient temples that still radiated dark power, where some fanatics still dwelt.

Thousands of years before the Replica Wars, the Sentari Order was fractured by a schism that began when a rogue Sentari and his followers turned away from the bright current of the Synth Flux, believing the shadow current was the path to true power. Having relocated to the planet Morthal in the Far Fringe Territories, their movement ultimately evolved into the Vorrak Order, resulting in a war between the former brothers and sisters in the Synth Flux as the Sentari Knights rallied to the defense of the Old Concord. Although the Vorrak were driven off of the Inner Reach world Aurelis by 5000 BBO, centuries of conflict ensued between the two orders, culminating in the Old Concord's collapse. Undermined by constant infighting and ruined by the last war against the Sentari, the Vorrak were rendered all but extinct with the exception of one; Xarn Bastor survived the ancient order's destruction and passed his knowledge to a single apprentice, Xarn Zelith, before being entombed on Morthal, which became known as Morthal. Henceforth, the Vorrak was governed by the Law of Pairs, a new philosophy dictating that only two Vorrak Lords - a Vorrak Master and a Vorrak apprentice - could exist at any given time. As a result, the Vorrak were all but forgotten while the Sentari focused their efforts on preserving the new age of peace under the modern Stellar Concord.

The Vorrak remained in hiding for a millennium until the rise of Xarn Morvath and Xarn Krell in 32 BBO. Morvath, trained by Xarn Pyrros, hid in plain sight as Senator Corvin Drexmar of Velora. Orchestrating the blockade and invasion of his homeworld by the Merchant Combine, Morvath maneuvered the Stellar Senate into electing him to the office of Supreme Chancellor. His entire chancellery would encompass the final years of the Concord. Over the next decade Morvath sowed the seeds of discontent within the galactic populace with the help of his new apprentice, the former Sentari Master Count Ralvek, who adopted the name of Xarn Taloth and became the public face of the Independent Movement for Self-Determination. The Secessionist Crisis culminated in the formation of the League of Free Worlds and the creation of the Secessionist Automaton Army which, in turn, compelled the Senate to support the establishment of the Grand Army of the Concord. Pol Pol Drubb, acting in place of Senator Avarre, started a motion to grant the Supreme Chancellor emergency powers.
