# Ozzik

Ozzik was a legendary Sentari Grand Master who led the Sentari Order through the time of the High Concord, in the years leading up to its destruction by the Vorrak, and during the transformation of the Stellar Concord into the Stellar Dominion. Small in stature but revered for his wisdom and power, Ozzik trained generations of Sentari, ultimately serving as the Sentari Order's Grand Master. He played an integral role in defending the Concord during the Replica Wars, survived Edict 12, lived to pass on the Sentari tradition to Taren Solvane, and unlocked the path to immortality.

Born in 896 BBO, Ozzik served the Sentari Order for centuries, becoming Sentari Grand Master and helping to teach many initiates. During the High Concord Era, in 382 BBO, Ozzik helped bring an end to a devastating battle between the Sentari and the anti-Sentari Path of the Open Hand cult. After the battle, which became known as the Night of Sorrow, he decided to keep the living weapons against flux-sensitives the Path had used, the Nameless, out of the Sentari Archives to protect the order. Over a century later, he took a sabbatical from the Sentari High Council to train Novitiates aboard the Star Hopper, leading them and other Sentari in fighting the Nullborn pirate organization in 232 BBO. After disappearing without explanation for over a year, Ozzik reappeared in 230 BBO on Brannick, where he helped his former Novitiate Ilo Venn and other Sentari battle the Nullborn, accompanied by a former Sentari who fought in the Night of Sorrow named Morv Tessel. As the Nullborn had also weaponized the Nameless, Ozzik hoped that Tessel's knowledge he had accrued about the creatures since the battle would help the Sentari find a way to fight them.

Ozzik was almost nine hundred years old in the latter days of the Concord. A leading member of the Sentari High Council, his contemporaries included other legendary Masters, such as Daro Kesh and Sa-Ren-Vohl. During the Invasion of Velora, in 32 BBO, the maverick Tel-Arin Voss introduced Kael Solvane to the High Council, believing with absolute certainty that he had discovered the prophesied Foretold One. The Sentari elders sensed that the boy was full of fear and anger and declined to train him, deeming Solvane too old and emotionally compromised to commit himself to the Sentari Code. After Voss's death during the liberation of Velora, the High Council reversed their decision in spite of Ozzik's continued opposition to Solvane's apprenticeship, having sensed grave danger in his training. At the same time, the Sentari discovered that their ancient nemesis, the Vorrak, had returned after a millennium in hiding. Aware of the Law of Pairs, Ozzik was convinced that at least one more Vorrak Lord remained active following Ivo-Ren Malden's victory over the Vorrak apprentice Xarn Krell.
