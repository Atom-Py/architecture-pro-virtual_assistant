# TVX/ln space superiority starfighter

The TVX/ln space superiority starfighter, also called the TVX/ln or simply the TVX fighter, was the signature starfighter of the Stellar Dominion and symbol of its space superiority. Instantly recognizable from the roar of its engines as well as its unique design, the TVX/ln exuded Dominion power and prestige across the galaxy, seeing use throughout the Dominion's reign. They were nicknamed Evil-Eyes or Eye-balls by members of the New Concord.

The TVX/ln starfighter was a small, short-range fighter developed, manufactured, and mass produced by Kestrel Fleet Systems (KFS). Developed by KFS company head Dorran Kestrel, the basis for the TVX fighter could be traced back to Drahl Systems Engineering's Beta-5 Cirrus starfighter, along with several other outmoded models from the Old Concord. Like their predecessors, TVX's employed two vertical wings similar in appearance to W-blade starfighters; however, the Veil-3 Sabre-class light interceptor bore even more similarities with its central cockpit pod, twin ion engines, and common weapons technology. Its official production name was the Twin Vector Exhaust "line edition" space superiority starfighter.

The fighter's name is derived from its propulsion system, which was specifically designed to create a distinct noise so as to inspire fear in those who opposed the Dominion. These were originally two P-s3 ion engines, which would be replaced by twin P-s4 ion engines by approximately 14 BBO. These engines could expel charged particles along almost any vector, providing the TVX fighter with incredible maneuverability, and their lack of moving parts or high-temperature components made them easier to maintain: Dominion records indicated less than five percent of all TVX fighters lost were as a result of field attrition. Fuel for the engines consisted of a radioactive gas kept in pressurized fuel tankage located on the underside of the cockpit.

A TVX fighter's twin ion engines, combined with W-401 maneuvering jets, provided thrust and boosters capable of quickly adjusting the ship's direction. The TVX/ln featured vertical takeoff and landing (VTOL) capabilities owing to grav-lift cyclers in its wing struts - reducing its already small mass to negligible weight - and micropositioning thrusts from the twin ion engines. Furthermore, the lack of a hyperdrive and resultant navigational systems alongside extended life support and fuel combined to cut down the TVX's total mass.

In order to reduce cost, mass and energy consumption while improving maneuverability, the TVX fighter lacked deflector shields. It was armored with tervanium steel plating which was proof against hand-held pulse guns (except in exposed areas, such as the stabilizers) but could be penetrated by direct hits from military-grade laser cannons, heavy pulse gun cannons, and micrometeor impacts. However the cockpit's spherical shape meant that glancing blows would be deflected away. TVX fighters were equipped with sensor jamming capabilities and could be retrofitted with additional electronic countermeasures.
