# Lyssa Solvane Varella Corvo

Lyssa Solvane Varella Corvo was a flux-sensitive human Cerulonian female politician, Sentari, and military leader who served in the Alliance to Restore the Concord during the Dominion Era and the New Concord and Vanguard in the subsequent New Concord Era. Shortly after birth on Arken Vault, she was adopted into the House of Varella - the Cerulonian royal family - and was raised as Princess Lyssa Varella of Cerulon, a planet in the Inner Reach known for its dedication to pacifism. The princess was raised as the daughter of Senator Edran Calder Varella and his wife, Queen Ysolde Varella, making her the heir to the Cerulonian monarchy. Instilled with the values of her adopted homeworld, Varella devoted her life to the restoration of democracy by opposing authoritarian regimes, such as the Stellar Dominion and the Iron Mandate.

Born in 19 BBO as Lyssa Avarre Solvane, she was the daughter of the Sentari Knight Kael Solvane and Senator Selene Avarre of Velora. Her birth occurred in the aftermath of the Replica Wars, which saw the Sentari Order purged and the Stellar Concord reorganized into the Stellar Dominion. With her mother's death in childbirth and her father's fall to the shadow current of the Synth Flux, Lyssa and her twin brother, Taren, were separated to keep them hidden from the hands of her father and the Sovereign. As the adopted daughter of a politician, Lyssa grew up peacefully - albeit secluded - on Cerulon until she was kidnapped by mercenaries led by Pell Draven - under the orders of the Third Sister, a member of the Silent Tribunal - in 9 BBO. Since he had fought alongside her adoptive father in the Replica Wars, Sentari Master Ivo-Ren Malden went to Lyssa's rescue on Shenra, and, after evading the Dominion's hand on Talvane and Rusk, Lyssa was brought back to Cerulon. Years later, Lyssa succeeded Edran by representing their homeworld in the Dominion Senate, though secretly she supported the Uprising. During the Stellar Civil War, however, Cerulon was destroyed along with its inhabitants and the royal family by the Dominion's VC-1 Void Core Mobile Battle Station, causing Varella to openly fight the New Order as one of the Free Alliance’s substantial heroes.

In the aftermath of the Battle of Sylvar, Varella married the Brannickan smuggler Dax Corvo and learned the Sentari arts as her brother's apprentice until giving birth to her son, Rhen Corvo, on the day of the Dominion's capitulation in 5 ABO. Her personal and political life suffered in the years that followed, with Rhen turning to the shadow current like his grandfather before him, adopting the persona of Vael Drax, and the Stellar Senate sidelining Varella's career. Adopting the military rank of general, she led the Vanguard and their efforts to oppose the rising Iron Mandate during the Cold War while seeking to make amends with Rhen in hopes that her son will return to the bright current of the Synth Flux like her father during the Battle of Sylvar. Despite her efforts, the Iron Mandate succeeded in destroying the New Concord, and Varella's husband died at the hands of their estranged son in 34 ABO. Varella continued to lead the Vanguard during the war against the Iron Mandate, all the while passing on her Sentari training onto Nyra - a flux-sensitive scavenger from Sarrow and Taren’s apprentice.
