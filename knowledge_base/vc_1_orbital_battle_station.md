# VC-1 Orbital Battle Station

The VC-1 Orbital Battle Station, also designated as the VC-1 Void Core Mobile Battle Station, referred to as the Ultimate Weapon in early development stages, later known as the Void Core I or the First Void Core, often simply referred to as the Void Core, and codenamed Stardust, was a moon-sized, deep-space mobile battle station constructed by the Stellar Dominion. It had been designed to fire a single planet-destroying superlaser focused by massive vael crystals which would destroy any planet targeted. It was the pet project of Sovereign Drexmar, Director Edwin Harl Strakos, and its eventual commander High Warden Aldric Vosk to expound the military philosophy of the aptly named Vosk Doctrine.

Some of the earliest plans for a mobile, planet-destroying superweapon dated back millennia to the ancient Vorrak. Thousands of years later, shortly before the outbreak of the Replica Wars, the Ferroxians, aligned with Count Ralvek and the League of Free Worlds, designed the Ultimate Weapon in secret. After the fall of the League, construction of the Void Core was appropriated by the nascent Dominion. It was built in orbit of Ferrox and supported by a complex logistical network of bases. The station, which took many years longer to complete than expected, was eventually moved from Ferrox to Pelagos in its final stages of construction.

The Axis network learned of the battle station's existence in a breach of Dominion Security Bureau files, and passed the knowledge through several operatives until it reached the Alliance to Restore the Concord at their secret base on Orrin 4. Though the Alliance was initially skeptical of the intel and its source, an unrelated Insurgent spy soon corroborated enough of the information for the Insurgents to begin an investigation. Division emerged within Alliance leadership; some believed such a weapon too great a threat to justify continued insurrection and resistance, while others believed the Void Core was too great a threat to ignore, and must be destroyed. Ultimately, rogue operatives Ilsa Reyl and Rafe Callen led a mission, which the Free Alliance soon joined, to steal the battle station's design plans, as Reyl had discovered that her father, who had been the primary engineer behind the station’s superweapon, had left a fatal flaw in the design, which could enable the Void Core to be destroyed with relative ease.

Having retrieved the Void Core plans from the Dominion Center of Military Research on Pelagos, Reyl was able to transmit them to the Insurgent fleet above the planet, and they were received by Princess Lyssa Varella of Cerulon aboard the Verity IV. Lyssa then fled through hyperspace toward Drassa, where she was apprehended by Xarn Velgor, but not before having hidden the plans in her navigator Q7-T4. While the Princess was taken prisoner, her automaton was able to escape and bring the plans to Ivo-Ren Malden, a former Sentari Knight living in hiding on Drassa.
