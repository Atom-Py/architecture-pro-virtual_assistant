# Murkveil

Murkveil was a remote, unexplored planet in the Murkveil system, and one of the purest places in the galaxy within the Synth Flux. A remote world of swamps and forests, it served as a refuge for Sentari Grand Master Ozzik during his exile after the destruction of the Sentari Order. Taren Solvane received advanced training in the ways of the Synth Flux under Sentari Master Ozzik on Murkveil, and it was later the place of Ozzik's death and transformation into the Synth Flux.

Murkveil was a planet located in the Vellis sector of the Far Fringe Territories. It was a hot and humid world that was a hotbed of life, with the Tessh species and numerous creatures - such as marshwings, swamp serpents, razorflies, mirelings, vine snakes, and swamp slugs - living on world. Sentari Master Ozzik would also make a home for himself on Murkveil during his exile. Murkveil was a mysterious, mist-shrouded, swampy planet, mostly covered in swamps and bogs, interspersed with countless shallow lakes and lagoons and multiple living caves. The continental and oceanic crusts of Murkveil were only vaguely defined, and there was little in the way of volcanic activity or earthquakes. Examples of flora included the lessia plant, the yoghurt plant, knotwood, and yarrel seed. The planet was devoid of any cities.

Among the purest locations in the entire galaxy within the energy field known as the Synth Flux, it shared a mysterious time-related quality with such locales as Kaylis and Ilun-Vey. The Cave of Evil was a confluence aligned with the shadow current of the Synth Flux on Murkveil. The Cave was able to expand its influence across Murkveil until Ozzik's arrival, which created a powerful presence in the bright current of the Synth Flux that kept the Cave in check. As such, during the Dominion Era, Murkveil's ecosystem saw balance until Ozzik's passing, which the Cave took advantage of to spread its darkness across Murkveil once more.

Murkveil had two seasons, a dry season and a wet season. During the peak of the long dry season (over half an orbit after the rains end) the uplands become too hot for most lifeforms to survive, necessitating any that live there to migrate to the lower lands. Beneath the rapidly leafing canopies of the knotwoods that emerge from the receding green and thick waters, the once-inundated lowlands revert back to a marshy swamp once again. As the air grows more humid, the gray overcast clouds return and before another quarter orbit passes the planet is far enough away from its sun that the dry season ends and the rains return, again flooding the lowlands and covering the trees in water, making the uplands habitable once more.

During the High Concord Era, an academic paper was written that described Murkveil as a world strong in the Synth Flux.
