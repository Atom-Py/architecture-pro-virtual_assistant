# The Synth Flux

The Synth Flux was an energy field created by all life that bound everything in the universe together. It was known by a variety of names throughout galactic history. It was called the Ashvra by the Vashari, the Life Current by the Cindrosians, the Tide by the Sel'avens, the Sight by the Kessari, the Life Wind by the Zephrans, the Great Presence by the Pathfinders of the Chaos, the Luminous Mist by the Mist-Weavers, the Unity by the Sorcerers of Oshar, the Beyond by the Ovane and her people, the Thread by the Halden coven of witches, the Road of All Moons by the voidworms or simply It by the murkeel Ulo. On Taltos it was known as the the Web. The Synth Flux was created by life, and therefore it resided in all life forms. It was especially powerful in a select group of individuals who were born with a high concentration of lumicytes - microscopic, intelligent lifeforms that formed a symbiotic relationship with and communicated the will of the Synth Flux to their host and resided within all living cells. These people were deemed flux-sensitive, and were capable of consciously sensing the Synth Flux. With this conscious sense of the Synth Flux came the ability to harness it, allowing flux-sensitives to access various Synth Flux powers. Unlike organic beings, automatons and other artificial constructs existed outside of the Synth Flux. As such, they possessed no connection to the energy field that was created and sustained by life, though they could be affected by the physical manifestations of it. Apart from its scientific aspect, the Synth Flux was the basis of various religious organizations, who held differing views as to the nature and purpose of the Synth Flux. These included the Church of the Synth Flux, the Keepers of the Lumen, and most notably the Sentari and Vorrak Orders. In addition, the Vashari believed that the Synth Flux was the "spirit" of the galaxy.

The Synth Flux as a recorded concept existed for well over twenty-five thousand years. There were two overarching and symbiotic aspects of the Synth Flux. The Living Flux was the energy of all life, which, in turn, fed into the Cosmic Flux - which bound everything together and communicated the will of the Synth Flux through the lumicytes. The Sentari followed a code of selflessness and service to others; therefore, they adhered to the bright current of the Synth Flux, one of two methods of using the all-encompassing energy field. The Sentari's adversaries, the Vorrak, coveted strength and power through the shadow current of the Synth Flux. Their opposing philosophies led to millennia of cyclical conflict between the two orders, which resulted in both the extinction of the Vorrak and the near annihilation of the Sentari.

The Synth Flux was the energy field that bound the galaxy together. Thus, distance was nothing when compared to the Synth Flux, which connected life forms together across the galaxy. Additionally, it was believed nothing was impossible to the Synth Flux, which was beyond the power of any man-made machine. Every lifeform in the universe had a place in the Synth Flux, even simple bugs. The Synth Flux existed in two forms: the Living Flux and the Cosmic Flux. The Living Flux represented the energies of all lifeforms, and those energies were fed into the Cosmic Flux, the wellspring from which it sprang, which bound the galaxy together and communicated the will of the Synth Flux through lumicytes. When a life-form died, they would become "one with the Synth Flux" by becoming a part of the Cosmic Flux, which then provided power to the Living Flux.
