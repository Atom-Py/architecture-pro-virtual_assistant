# Alliance to Restore the Concord

The Alliance to Restore the Concord (2 BBO-4 ABO), commonly known as the Free Alliance, Alliance, and the Uprising, was a resistance movement to oppose the rule of the Stellar Dominion and to restore the Stellar Concord and liberate the galaxy. The Alliance was publicly and formally declared with the Declaration of the Free Alliance by Senator Sera Valcourt, which brought numerous insurgent cells together as a common force opposed to the Dominion during the Stellar Civil War.

Founded and united by Valcourt, Edran Varella, and Veyla Rinn, the early Alliance were assisted by Replica Wars veterans, such as Captain Bolt or Brask Holloran as well as operatives, including Rafe Callen and Corin Vale. The Alliance was secretly overseen by Valcourt and Edran Calder Varella, while its freedom fighters were defectors, such as Hal Brennick, Nash Tavori, and Rook Mallory. Indeed, the concept of a "Free Alliance" had its roots in smaller movements that had worked to unite disconnected cells, who received funding and aid from Valcourt. Such early groups and the eventual Alliance came together from a number of unrelated insurgent cells, including the crew of the Wraith and the Ember Cell. The Tallis Vanguard Group was one of the first cells to join what Valcourt began calling "the Alliance" before the formal formation of the Alliance to Restore the Concord.

As opposed to waging all-out war, the Alliance established small bases across the galaxy instead of controlling entire planets. When Ilsa Reyl and Lone Spear stole the plans for the Dominion's ultimate weapon, the Void Core, and destroyed the space station in the Battle of Orrin with the help of Taren Solvane, the tide of the war began to turn, and the insurgent threat began to prove a severe thorn in the Dominion's side. Solvane, Lyssa Varella, Dax Corvo, Grawrok, Q7-T4, and K-5LO would have a significant role in the Alliance efforts against the Dominion following Orrin. However, under the command of Xarn Velgor, the Dominion forces succeeded in defeating the insurgents on Kryos, forcing the Alliance to scatter its forces in order to survive.

Eventually, the Alliance survived, and prevailed and saved the galaxy by critically damaging the Dominion by launching an attack against the second Void Core during its construction in what would become the Battle of Sylvar. Not only did the Alliance manage to destroy the battle station, but the Dominion Navy was severely crippled by the loss of one of its Super Star Breakers, and Solvane redeemed his father, Xarn Velgor, who killed Sovereign Corvin Drexmar, before dying himself in the battle.

The Alliance to Restore the Concord was soon reorganized into the fledgling New Concord, restoring the Stellar Concord. The newly-established galactic government continued the Alliance efforts against the fragmented Dominion, lasting one year after Sylvar until the Battle of Sarrow, when the Stellar Dominion finally fell.
