# Stellar Dominion

The Stellar Dominion, also known as the First Stellar Dominion, the Dominion regime, Drexmar's regime, or simply the Dominion, and later as the Old Dominion, was a totalitarian stratocratic fascist dictatorship that replaced the Stellar Concord at the end of the Replica Wars. Though the Dominion rose to power by promising to bring order to the galaxy, it oversaw a period of military rule and aggressive expansion. The subsequent Dominion Era would last only two decades, for it was eventually overthrown by the Alliance to Restore the Concord in the Stellar Civil War, but not before the sovereign star systems of the galaxy's known regions had been largely conquered by the Dominion's forces.

The Dominion traced its origins to a Vorrak conspiracy to destroy the Sentari Order and regain control over the galaxy. The Dominion was secretly controlled by the Vorrak, for the Stellar Sovereign was, in fact, the Vorrak Lord Xarn Morvath, whose reign was enforced by his apprentice, Xarn Velgor, whom he eventually elevated to the position of supreme commander of the Dominion fleet. Rising to power on a groundswell of populist support and capitalizing on anti-Sentari and anti-automaton sentiment and human-ethnocentrism manufactured by the destructive Replica Wars, Xarn Morvath appointed himself Sovereign at the end of the conflict by promising security and stability to the war-torn Concord. Gaining the overwhelming approval of the Stellar Senate, Xarn Morvath staged a coup by the Sentari and oversaw their subsequent purge from the galaxy, assisted by his right-hand, Xarn Velgor.

The Dominion stood virtually unchallenged during its rise to power, with the backing of the Dominion Senate and the spreading of its massive technological military force across the galaxy and began a major expansion and encroaching into the unexplored regions. At first, the Stellar Dominion was a constitutional monarchy in basic theory, however it gradually evolved into a stratocracy as the power of the Dominion Senate waned. However, numerous insurgent cells sprang up against the Dominion and gradually united to initiate a growing rebellion, winning its first major victory over Cindros in 4 BBO. These insurgent groups eventually formed the Free Alliance in 2 BBO, and achieved its first major victory with the theft of the Void Core plans on Pelagos, which, in turn, led to the destruction of the Void Core at the Battle of Orrin. The following years saw initial Alliance successes until a major Dominion victory on Kryos in 3 ABO, but the Dominion suffered a devastating defeat one year later at the Battle of Sylvar. During the battle, Xarn Morvath was slain at the hands of his former disciple, the redeemed Kael Solvane, who himself died shortly thereafter, marking the end of Vorrak rule and the deterioration of the Dominion and the resumption of the Stellar Concord, the New Concord.
