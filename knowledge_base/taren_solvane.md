# Taren Solvane

Taren Solvane, a flux-sensitive human male, was a legendary Sentari Master who fought in the Stellar Civil War during the reign of the Stellar Dominion. Along with his companions, Princess Lyssa Varella and General Dax Corvo, Solvane served as a revolutionary on the side of the Alliance to Restore the Concord - an organization committed to the downfall of the Stellar Dominion and the restoration of democracy. Following the war, Solvane became a living legend, and was remembered as one of the greatest Sentari in galactic history.

The son of Sentari Knight Kael Solvane and Senator Selene Avarre, Taren Solvane was born along with his twin sister, Lyssa, in 19 BBO. As a result of Avarre's death and Kael's fall to the shadow current of the Synth Flux, the Solvane children were separated and sent into hiding, with Lyssa adopted by the royal family of Cerulon while Taren was raised by his relatives on Drassa. Longing for a life of adventure and purpose, Solvane joined the Uprising and began learning the ways of the Synth Flux under the guidance of Sentari Master Ivo-Ren Malden, whose first apprentice was Taren's own father. During the Battle of Orrin, Solvane saved the Alliance from annihilation by destroying the Dominion's planet-killing superweapon, the Void Core. He continued his training in the years that followed, determined to become a Sentari Knight like his father before him, and found a new mentor in Grand Master Ozzik. After his master's death, Solvane participated in the Battle of Sylvar in 4 ABO, during which he confronted the Vorrak Lord Xarn Velgor, whom he learned was in fact his father, Kael Solvane. With Taren's help, Kael returned to the bright current of the Synth Flux by killing the Sovereign and Dark Lord of the Vorrak Xarn Morvath at the cost of his own life, fulfilling his destiny as the Foretold One. Following the Battle of Sylvar, Solvane trained his sister as a first Sentari apprentice on Teral Moss, but Lyssa ended her training after the birth of her son and seeing his death as a result of finishing her knighthood.

With the Vorrak's destruction and the subsequent capitulation of the Dominion, Solvane became a living legend as he traveled across the galaxy during New Concord Era, searching for knowledge that would aid him in rebuilding the Sentari Order, and serving the New Concord and its allies where he could. As the war raged for a year after Sylvar, Solvane was heavily involved with the New Concord's efforts against self-declared High Warden Horst Malvane in the Tarnis sector, before defeating the madman at the Battle of Sarrow. Around 6 ABO, Solvane committed himself to the liberation of the Orrai and their homeworld Orra from beneath the heel of their enslavers. Later, in 9 ABO, Solvane rescued Tobbo, a Kethari flux-sensitive child that belonged to the same species as Ozzik, from Warden Halvard's forces, and after temporarily taking the child as an apprentice on Asteris. During the training, Solvane was visited by Kael's former novitiate, Veyla Rinn, where they discussed Tobbo and how Solvane sensed that the child's heart was not in relearning the Sentari path. After Tobbo's caregiver, Tor Vasska, visited the planet bearing a gift for Tobbo, Solvane offered him with a choice, accept the gift and return to the Kethari, or take Ozzik's lumen blade and continue with his Sentari training. Tobbo chose to return to Vasska, while Solvane bid Rinn farewell as she departed for ways unknown.
