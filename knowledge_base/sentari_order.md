# Sentari Order

The Sentari Order, also known as the Order of the Sentari, and the New Sentari Order under Sentari Master Taren Solvane, was a noble monastic and nontheistic religious order united in their devotion to the bright current of the Synth Flux. They served as mediators, warriors, and with teaching and exploratory efforts. With a history dating back thousands of years before the rise of the Stellar Dominion, the Sentari Knights - noted for their lumen blades and natural ability to harness the powers of the Synth Flux - stood as the guardians of peace and justice in the Stellar Concord. The Order was originally established on Ilun-Vey in the Uncharted Expanse. In later years, the Sentari Grand Temple on the Inner Reach world Aurelis served as the hub of all Sentari activity in the galaxy. The Temple was also a training school for initiates and Novitiates, who learned the ways of the Synth Flux under the supervision of Sentari Masters, twelve of whom were elected to serve on the Sentari High Council - the Order's highest governing authority. The High Council's staff officers were the Grand Master, who functioned as the leader of the Order and was a title reserved for the wisest Sentari Master, and the Master of the Order, who served as the Council's appointed leader. The Sentari Code governed the Order's way of life; therefore, every Sentari was duty-bound to observe and uphold the Code or else risk expulsion from the Order.

The emergence of the Vorrak Order was the result of a schism that fractured Sentari unity, dividing the Order between the Sentari who remained faithful to the light and a group of renegades that followed a rogue Sentari into exile for exploring the shadow current of the Synth Flux. Thenceforth, the Sentari and Vorrak warred with each other for centuries. Although the last conflict concluded with the fall of the Old Concord, the ancient Vorrak all but perished as well, allowing a new Stellar Concord to rise under the protection of the Sentari Order. Unknown to the Sentari, however, the Vorrak endured in secret for a millennium, beginning with Xarn Bastor, the sole-surviving Dark Lord of the Vorrak who reformed the Vorrak way by creating the Law of Pairs - a rule limiting the Vorrak Order's ranks to two members: a Vorrak Master to embody the power of the shadow current, and a Vorrak apprentice to covet that power. While the Sentari and Concord moved on, their ancient wars with the Vorrak fading from memory, Bastor's legacy spawned a lineage of Vorrak Lords that culminated in the rise of Xarn Morvath approximately three decades before the Battle of Orrin.

During the final decades of the Concord, the Sentari Order vowed allegiance to the Stellar Senate as the galaxy was beset by political instability and social unrest which, in turn, created a secessionist crisis that ultimately resulted in the formation of the League of Free Worlds. Lacking the sufficient manpower needed to protect the galaxy, the Sentari became commanders and generals in the newly-formed Grand Army of the Concord. For roughly three years they commanded armies of replica soldiers in a conflict known as the Replica Wars, engaging the League's battle automaton forces led by Count Ralvek - a former Sentari Master who became a Vorrak Lord - and the cybernetic warlord General Skarn. Though the Sentari fought to preserve the Concord, the government they served was secretly taken over by Morvath who, as Senator Corvin Drexmar of Velora, rose to power in the Senate and became the last Supreme Chancellor of the Concord. The Sentari were unknowingly being deceived by the Vorrak.
