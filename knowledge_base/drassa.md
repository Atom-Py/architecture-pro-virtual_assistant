# Drassa

Drassa was a sparsely inhabited circumbinary desert planet located in the galaxy's Far Fringe Territories. Part of a binary star system, the planet orbited two scorching suns, resulting in the world lacking the necessary surface water to sustain large populations. As a result, many residents of the planet instead drew water from the atmosphere via vapor wells. The planet also had little surface vegetation. It was the homeworld to the native Skiv and Varrak Raider species and of Kael and Taren Solvane, who would go on to shape galactic history.

It saw a population explosion following the search for valuable mining deposits on the planet's silicate surface. Finding the ore to have undesirable metallurgical properties, mining firms left the planet en masse, leaving behind valuable equipment, most notably large dune crawlers, which were later used by the Skiv population. The planet then became a haven for smugglers and criminal activity, and fell under the influence of the Gruuk Clan while scattered, isolated settlements of hard-working inhabitants endured.

Generally ignored by the galaxy at large, Drassa gained the notice of the Stellar Dominion during the Stellar Civil War when an escape pod carrying two automatons in possession of top-secret information landed on the sandy Far Fringe world. The Dominion went on to establish a heavier presence on the planet, forging a tenuous relationship with Gorrba Velikor Thuun of the Gruuk Clan. Open conflict soon broke out between the Dominion and a Free Alliance cell on Drassa that culminated in a battle that left the Dominion's hold on the world significantly weakened.

Following the death of Gorrba and the withdrawal of Dominion forces after the Battle of Sylvar, an enormous power vacuum occurred on the world, with numerous crime syndicates like the Red Key Raiders attempting to build legitimacy as mining companies. Eventually, former bounty hunter Jorek Strand returned and took over Gorrba's former territory. Coming into conflict with both the Gruuk Clan and the Vorne Syndicate, the new Overlord was pulled into a gang war that threatened the future of Drassa. With help from the Kethari Tor Vasska, Strand rallied the denizens of the settlements Kel Varra and Freehold to push back against the invaders, ushering in a new era of respect between the people of the planet.

Drassa was a hot desert planet located in the Far Fringe, a region of the galaxy far removed from its core. It had three moons, Gorrasa, Vermessa, and Kesh Ruu. Drassa orbited twin suns, Drass I and Drass II. According to Skiv folklore, the desert known as the Ashen Sea was once a true ocean. The whole planet was covered in harsh deserts, and only a small part of its northern hemisphere could sustain intelligent life. The landscape was covered with sand dunes, mountains, and canyons, which made traversing the planet very difficult. Due to the absence of surface water, those who lived on Drassa had to draw moisture through the dry air.
