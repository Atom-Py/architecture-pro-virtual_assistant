# R-40C Z-blade starfighter

The R-40C Z-blade starfighter, also known as the R-40C space superiority fighter, or R-40C Z-blade multi-role starfighter, was a single-seat craft manufactured by Orrel Corporation and used most famously by the Alliance to Restore the Concord against the Stellar Dominion. Renowned for its speed and maneuverability during the Stellar Civil War, it became the backbone of the Free Alliance Starfighter Corps, being both harder hitting and tougher under fire than its main adversary, the mass-produced TVX/ln space superiority starfighter of the Dominion Navy.

Years following the destruction of the Stellar Dominion and the establishment of the New Concord, the R-40C was initially retained by the New Concord Defense Fleet before eventually being replaced by newer Z-blade models, such as the R-50 Z-blade starfighter, and the R-60 Z-blade.

The R-40C Z-blade starfighter, referred to as the R-40C space superiority fighter, or R-40C Z-blade multi-role starfighter, was manufactured by Orrel Corporation. It was designed to excel in all aspects of starfighter combat, being highly maneuverable and heavily shielded, with a small profile that made it harder to hit. Unlike other starfighters the Z-blade could excel in atmospheric flight. This included outmaneuvering the TVX fighter, which was otherwise faster and more maneuverable than the Z-blade in space. The Z-blade was classified as a starfighter, specifically a snub-fighter due to its size. The starship weighed 18,000 kg.

It had two pairs of wing-like strike foils, or strike-vanes, mounted at the rear of the craft on opposite sides. Normally, the foils on each side locked in place flush against each other; during combat, however, the foils were folded out. This gave the craft its distinctive "X"-like appearance when viewed from the front or rear. The strike-vanes were controlled by a strike-vane servo actuator located in the back of the starfighter. With the strike-vanes open in attack position, the craft had a greater spread of fire as opposed to whilst shut.

The cockpit had flight controls similar to those of the R-12 skyskipper, an aircar also produced by Orrel Corporation, along with a targeting computer with holographic imaging system. Under the Z-blade was a cargo compartment, a grav-lift, and three reinforced landing legs to protect the craft from rough landings and take-offs. While listed with a maximum atmospheric speed of 1,050 kph, the Z-blade could otherwise fly at supersonic speeds. Indeed, with the engines at maximum thrust a Z-blade could travel so fast it would glow hot from atmospheric friction. Doing so put tremendous strain on the starfighter and the pilot themselves however: after just several minutes of maneuvering at these velocities, gradual system failures began and even the cannons themselves were at risk of melting from the heat. When traveling high in a planet's upper atmosphere, a Z-blade could achieve speeds fast enough to cause the pilot's head to swim and their vision to glitter.
